MarkdownNote is a clean, distraction-free note-taking app built around Markdown. Whether you're jotting down quick ideas or writing structured documents, MarkdownNote keeps your notes organized and beautifully formatted.

■ Powerful Markdown Editor
Write in plain Markdown with full CommonMark support, including GitHub Flavored Markdown extensions — tables and strikethrough included. A placeholder hint guides first-time users right from the start.

■ Three Editor Modes
Switch between modes to match your workflow:
• Edit — focus entirely on writing
• Split — see your Markdown source and rendered preview side by side in real time
• View — read your note as a polished, fully rendered document

■ Instant Search
Find any memo in seconds. Just start typing and results filter live as you go.

■ Swipe to Delete
Swipe a memo left to remove it. A confirmation step prevents accidental deletions.

■ File Export
Share or back up your notes in three formats:
• Plain Text (.txt)
• Markdown (.md)
• HTML (.html)
Files are saved directly to your device's Documents folder, ready to open in any app.

■ Offline & Private
All notes are stored locally on your device using a lightweight SQLite database. No account, no internet connection, no data ever leaves your phone.

■ Material Design 3
Built with the latest Material You design system for a modern, clean look that feels right at home on any Android device.
